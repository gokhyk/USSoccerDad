# Coach Quick Reference Guide

## Game Day Checklist

### Before the Game
1. ✅ Open your team's game list
2. ✅ Select today's game
3. ✅ Tap game to open availability screen
4. ✅ Check off players who are present
5. ✅ Choose substitution frequency (Frequent/Balanced/Infrequent)
6. ✅ Tap "Start Game"

### Pre-Game Screen
- Review the calculated starting lineup
- Starters are chosen by who has played the least this season
- When ready, tap **"Blow Whistle"** to start

### During the Game

#### What You'll See
- **Large clock** showing current period time
- **Green dots** next to players on the field
- **Gray dots** next to players on the bench
- Each player's playing time displayed

#### Substitution Countdown
**60 seconds before each sub:**
- Overlay appears showing who comes in/out
- **Green** countdown (>30 sec)
- **Orange** countdown (15-30 sec)  
- **Red** countdown (<15 sec)

**When countdown reaches 0:**
- If auto-complete is ON: Players swap automatically
- If auto-complete is OFF: Tap "Substitution Complete" button

#### Period Breaks
- Countdown shows remaining break time
- **Red** color when <15 seconds remain
- Preview of next period's starting lineup
- Tap "Start Period X" when ready (unless auto-start is enabled)

### Post-Game Screen
- View final playing time for all players
- See percentage of game played
- Tap **"Share"** to send stats via text/email
- Tap **"Save & Exit"** to update season totals and return

## Settings Explained

**Tap the gear icon ⚙️ during game for settings:**

### Auto-complete substitutions
- **ON**: Players swap automatically at scheduled time
- **OFF**: You must tap "Substitution Complete" button
- *Recommended for busy coaches*

### Auto-start after break
- **ON**: Next period starts automatically when break ends
- **OFF**: You must tap "Start Period" button
- *Recommended for consistent game flow*

### Skip break countdown
- **ON**: No countdown shown, must manually start period
- **OFF**: Shows countdown timer during breaks
- *Use if you want full control of timing*

## Substitution Frequency Guide

### Frequent
**Best for:** Very young players (U5-U7), hot weather, high-intensity games
- U5-U7: 3 subs per 10-min period (every ~2.5 min)
- U8+: 4 subs per period (every ~5-6 min)

### Balanced ⭐ (Recommended)
**Best for:** Most situations, typical game conditions
- U5-U7: 2 subs per 10-min period (every ~3 min)
- U8+: 3 subs per period (every ~7-8 min)

### Infrequent
**Best for:** Older players (U12+), cooler weather, lower intensity
- U5-U7: 1 sub per 10-min period (at 5 min)
- U8+: 2 subs per period (every ~10 min)

## Player Arrival Tips

### Late Player Arrives
1. Tap the gear icon ⚙️
2. Navigate back to availability
3. Check the late player's box
4. Return to game
5. They'll be credited with expected playing time
6. They'll enter in the next substitution rotation

### Player Leaves Early
- Their statistics are saved as-is
- Other players will get slightly more playing time
- No action needed from coach

## Understanding Player Stats

### During Game Display
**Example: "12:30 (4:15 cont.)"**
- **12:30** = Total time played this game
- **4:15 cont.** = Time in current stint on field (resets after sub)

### Why Continuous Time Matters
Players with the **highest continuous time** come out first (they're most tired).

### Post-Game Stats
**Example: "23:45 (87%)"**
- **23:45** = Total time played
- **87%** = Percentage of total game time

## Common Questions

**Q: Why isn't a player starting?**  
A: Starters are chosen by season playing time. Players who've played less start first to keep things fair.

**Q: Can I manually substitute a player?**  
A: Not in the current version. The app ensures fair, automatic rotation based on playing time.

**Q: What if I need to adjust the lineup?**  
A: For now, use the automatic system. Future versions may add manual override.

**Q: A sub is scheduled at 9:30 in a 10-minute period?**  
A: The app automatically skips subs within 90 seconds of period end.

**Q: How do I share statistics with parents?**  
A: After the game, tap "Share" button to send via text, email, or save to files.

## Tips for Success

### Pre-Season Setup
- Enter all players with accurate jersey numbers
- Make sure season totals are correct (if mid-season)

### Game Day
- Arrive 15 minutes early to confirm attendance
- Use "Balanced" frequency for most games
- Enable auto-complete for smoother game flow

### Post-Game
- Always tap "Save & Exit" to update season statistics
- Share stats with parents immediately while fresh

### For Tournament Days
- Complete each game fully before starting the next
- Stats will accumulate across all games
- Review total playing time between games

## Troubleshooting

**Clock isn't starting:**
- Make sure you tapped "Blow Whistle"
- Check that at least minimum players are marked present

**Substitution not happening:**
- Check if auto-complete is enabled
- Tap "Substitution Complete" if manual mode

**Can't share statistics:**
- Make sure you're on the post-game screen
- Try tapping "Share" again
- Check app permissions for sharing

**Player stats look wrong:**
- Verify they were marked present before game
- Check if they were marked late (they get credited time)
- Ensure you saved previous games properly
